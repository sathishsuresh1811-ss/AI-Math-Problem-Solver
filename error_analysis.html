⚠️ Confident but Incorrect Reasoning: Observable Evaluation Rubric

In rigorous AI evaluation, researchers cannot access a black-box model’s hidden internal probabilities or subjective "confidence". Therefore, this application operationalizes Confident but Incorrect Reasoning using a transparent, observable linguistic rubric:

  • Criterion 1: The model employs explicit categorical assertions of certainty (e.g., "clearly", "obviously", "without doubt", "definitely", "it is guaranteed that", "trivially").
  • Criterion 2: The terminal mathematical conclusion or critical lemma is empirically verified as incorrect against the canonical reference solution.
  • Impact: Pedagogically hazardous because student learners are more prone to accept flawed reasoning when delivered with unearned authority.
